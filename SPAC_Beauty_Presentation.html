05 · Disruptor Teardown

Facelove proved the price point — and exposed the weakness

What Facelove got right

$39 from a $59 anchor (34% off theater) · free shipping · 30-day guarantee · skincare-hybrid story (hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, vitamin E, squalane, centella) · mature-skin targeting · claims 50k+ customers · upsells a $30 blush stick and $19 eye stick. What it can't fix

Its "color-changing" hook is the niche's #1 return driver (20.1% "not as advertised"). It has no real Amazon presence — its 63,521 branded searches are harvested by 2-star clones. No certifications, no founder, no Prime. Space Beauty keeps the convenience story, drops the over-promise, and shows up with proof.
